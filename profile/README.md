## First Look at ProgDVB Professional

ProgDVB Professional is for people who keep a DVB device they own and want one named prog desk, not mixed untitled tuners. It gives you an own tuner, a channel desk, and a short path back to a capture list you already keep. It does not replace the rest of your PC. It sits beside the device you already use.

Work stays on a tuner you keep. Extra devices wait for their own title.
